**Artist Statement**
For Project 2, I decided to use a spiral staircase because I liked the way it looked from above. I chose blue and pink for the steps since those were two of the colors available for Risograph printing. I used randomness to change the size, angle, and spacing of the steps. I wanted the steps to look a little different each time, but still keep the staircase recognizable. I also left the background white because I thought it looked better with those two colors.
